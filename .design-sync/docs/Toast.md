Transient notification. Render `Toast` (variant `default`/`success`/`warning`/`destructive`) with `ToastTitle` + `ToastDescription`. In the app, use `ToastProvider` + the `useToast()` hook to queue them; `destructive` toasts persist until dismissed. Keep copy factual — state what happened and the next step.
