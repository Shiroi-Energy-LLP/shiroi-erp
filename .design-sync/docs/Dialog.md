Centered modal for focused tasks and confirmations. Compose `Dialog` › `DialogTrigger` + `DialogContent` (`DialogHeader` › `DialogTitle` + `DialogDescription`, then body, then `DialogFooter` with actions). Dims the page with a blurred overlay. Use for confirm/approve flows; keep the title a question and the primary action a verb.
