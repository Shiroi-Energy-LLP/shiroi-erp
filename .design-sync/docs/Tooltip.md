Brief hint on hover/focus. Wrap the app (or the region) in `TooltipProvider`, then compose `Tooltip` › `TooltipTrigger` (`asChild`) + `TooltipContent`. Dark `n-900` bubble. Use for glossary hints and icon-button labels — keep it to a short phrase, never essential information.
